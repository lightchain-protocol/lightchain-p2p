Secure your account

Your account lives on this machine. If you lose it - a broken laptop, a reinstall - twelve words are the only way back in. Nobody can reissue them for you.

Choose a password

This encrypts the wallet on this machine and is asked for each time the app opens. It is not your recovery phrase, and it cannot be reset - but you can always start again with your phrase.

    Restore your wallet

    Twelve words, in order. Capitals and extra spaces do not matter.

    This replaces the wallet on this machine. Rooms and messages already here stay on disk. If this phrase is the wallet they belong to, they come back.

    Twelve words.

    My phrase also has a passphrase

    Some wallets add a word of your own on top of the twelve, sometimes called a passphrase or a 25th word. Only fill this in if you set one. Capitals and spaces matter here, and nothing can tell you it is wrong - the wrong one quietly opens a different, empty wallet.

    Welcome back

    Unlock the wallet on this machine to continue.

    This wallet's file cannot be read It is still on this machine and nothing has been changed. No password will open it while it is in this state. Restore the file from a backup, or use “Forgotten your password?” to set this wallet up again from its recovery phrase.

    Getting back in

    The password cannot be recovered or reset - it never left this machine and is not stored anywhere. What you can do depends on what you still have.

    Remove this wallet

    Its password confirms this is yours to remove. You can then create a new wallet or restore a different one.

    Make sure your recovery phrase is written down first. Removing the wallet here does not delete anything held by it, but the phrase is the only way to reach it again.

    Start over

    Without the password or the recovery phrase there is no way into the wallet on this machine - not here, and not with any other tool. Starting over gives you a working application with a new, empty wallet.

    Anything the old wallet holds becomes unreachable forever. No balance, no rooms you are the only member of, and no messages only it can decrypt. Rooms and messages stay on disk and are not deleted. If you ever find the phrase, all of it comes back.
