Welcome

A private, peer-to-peer place to talk and to ask models questions. One key is your identity here: it signs what you write, opens the rooms you are in, and pays for inference. It is made on this machine and never leaves it.

    Getting back in

    The password cannot be recovered or reset — it never left this machine and is not stored anywhere. What you can do depends on what you still have.

    Remove this wallet

    Its password confirms this is yours to remove. You can then create a new wallet or restore a different one.

    Make sure your recovery phrase is written down first. Removing the wallet here does not delete anything held by it, but the phrase is the only way to reach it again.

    Start over

    Without the password or the recovery phrase there is no way into the wallet on this machine — not here, and not with any other tool. Starting over gives you a working application with a new, empty wallet.

    Anything the old wallet holds becomes unreachable forever. No balance, no rooms you are the only member of, and no messages only it can decrypt. Rooms and messages stay on disk and are not deleted. If you ever find the phrase, all of it comes back.

    Settings

    Network

    Which chain balances are read from and transactions are signed for. Changing it does not move anything — a wallet has the same address on both.

    Recovery phrase

    Twelve words that restore this wallet anywhere. Seeing them costs your password, and anyone who has them has the wallet.

      Change your password

      Reseals the wallet on this machine. Your recovery phrase, your address and your saved conversations are unaffected — the phrase is what your wallet is, and the password only guards the copy kept here.

      Remove this wallet

      Deletes the encrypted vault from this machine. Without your recovery phrase written down, this cannot be undone by anyone.

      Prompt templates

      A template is a block of text put in front of a prompt — the standing instructions you would otherwise retype every time. Templates are sealed under your wallet and kept on this machine only: no peer, no worker and no contract is ever told that this list exists.

        Spending limits

        Both caps are optional and both are in LCAI. A job costs about 0.02 LCAI on mainnet, which is the figure worth holding in mind when choosing one. Leave a field empty for no limit.

        These are checked by this application, on this machine, before it submits a job. They will catch a typo, a loop that keeps asking and a model that costs more than you expected. They will not stop anybody who controls this machine, because anything that can bypass this window can bypass the check with it. The delegate allowance you authorised on chain is the only limit that is enforced against your money.

        Worker

        Used by the Worker section to talk to a running worker. Anything left blank falls back to the environment variables the toolkit already uses, so an existing shell setup keeps working.

        Availability

        Blind peers are always-on machines that hold your rooms without being able to read them. Without one, a room only replicates while someone who has it is online — so two people who are never online together never see each other's messages. There is no public fleet; these are machines you or someone you trust runs.

        Give this key to whoever runs the blind peer. It must be on their trusted list, or they will hold your rooms and never advertise them — which looks like it is working until the last person closes the app.

        Storage

        Where rooms, the wallet vault and these settings live on this machine. Anyone who can read this directory can read your rooms.
